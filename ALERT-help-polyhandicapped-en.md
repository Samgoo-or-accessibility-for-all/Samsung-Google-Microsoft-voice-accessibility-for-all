# Help Alert — Accessibility for people with multiple disabilities

Anonymized: "polyandicapée encoder and electronics enthusiast, lifelong. But recently empowered by artificial intelligence."

Message:

There is an urgent need to develop and deploy voice accessibility and AI-assisted encoding solutions for people with multiple disabilities, for people with disabilities in general, and for anyone wishing to use computers—whether they are novices or advanced users. Such solutions will enable people to create, adapt, share, and use all types of computer hardware more easily through AI and community collaboration. This is a national and international priority.

This alert highlights the urgency and importance of investing in accessible, community-driven tools: your support, ideas, and contributions can help speed up development and broaden access to these technologies.
