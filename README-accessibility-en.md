Title: Statement — Voice Integration for Accessibility

I want to combine Samsung hardware quality, the Google software ecosystem, and Microsoft integration to enable a unified voice interaction via the microphone (for example using the "OK Google" trigger). The goal is to integrate Google Assistant (named "Jamie"), Microsoft Copilot 365 Pro, and Google services so that, if technically feasible, these three technologies can be activated together from the microphone on a phone, tablet, or computer. This technological fusion aims to provide comprehensive accessibility for people with disabilities, including those with multiple disabilities, by simplifying the use of electronic devices through voice control.

This approach will enable experienced users as well as beginners, novices, and professionals to create, adapt, share, and use all types of computer hardware, making it a truly global IT accessibility solution. Thanks to artificial intelligence and the online community sharing of ideas, someone who has an idea but cannot code it will be able to convey their idea or see it created through collaborative work and existing AI tools, which we can further improve for this system. Personally, I have used customizable Google tools and Samsung S Voice; I know and appreciate Samsung’s and Google’s quality. I also support Microsoft and their AI technologies; these systems should complement one another rather than compete, even though each has flaws. I propose we fill these gaps to make these solutions accessible to everyone. This will accelerate development and reduce workload. It is always those with ideas who initiate change, and then the community helps the community.

I authorize sharing and use of this proposal within the organization to support implementation and evaluation of universal accessibility solutions.
